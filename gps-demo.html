How the feature works

GPS tracking is optional and starts only when the driver enables it during an active shift. The location foreground service estimates distance while the driver uses another app or locks the screen. App and notification controls allow the driver to pause or stop tracking. Tracking does not automatically start for a new shift or after restoring a backup.

Position fixes are processed on the device; DashTrack does not save or upload route history. GPS can miss miles. Drivers must review their actual final odometer and business miles. Controls should only be used while safely parked.
