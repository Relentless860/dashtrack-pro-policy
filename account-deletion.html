Delete your DashTrack Pro account

Updated November 3, 2026

This page covers the optional email/password sign-in account in account-enabled Android 1.6.5 and later testing builds. It does not announce a public app release. It is not a request to delete your Google or delivery-platform account.

Request deletion without the app

Email dashtrackpro@gmail.com with the subject DashTrack Pro account deletion. Include the email address registered to your DashTrack account. Send from that address if possible.

Request account deletion

You do not need to reinstall the app or give a reason. If you cannot access the registered email, contact support and explain; additional ownership verification may be needed. Never send your password, verification code, recovery link or payment details.

How we handle the request

We review the request and verify control of the account before removing its Firebase sign-in record. Knowing an email address alone is not sufficient to request someone else's deletion. We will explain if further verification or a legitimate retention requirement affects the request. Support messages are handled by our email provider and retained only as reasonably needed for the request, recordkeeping or legal obligations.

What is deleted

Your DashTrack Firebase sign-in account, its account profile and ability to sign in with those credentials. Google processes removal from its live and backup systems under Firebase's documented retention and deletion practices; not all service logs or backups disappear immediately.

What stays on your device

This feature does not upload or attach shifts, mileage, receipts, earnings, settings or plans to a Firebase account. Those records are shared by the local app installation, not separated into private account profiles. Signing out or deleting a sign-in account does not hide or delete them. Support cannot remotely access or erase them.

Delete individual records in DashTrack, or clear app data in device settings to remove local installation data. Export anything you need first. Exports and backups you shared elsewhere are not remotely deleted by this request.

Delete from inside DashTrack

While safely parked with no active shift, open Driver settings → Account → Delete account. Enter your current password, type DELETE and confirm. Guest tracking remains available without creating another account.

Subscription data and cancellation

In current free beta builds, paid subscriptions are not enabled and RevenueCat connections remain disabled. If you use a future billing-enabled version, RevenueCat subscription records are separate from the optional Firebase email account. Deleting the Firebase account or local app data does not automatically delete RevenueCat records.

In a billing-enabled version, Remember billing connection saves only an on/off choice in encrypted Android device storage; it does not store a premium entitlement or upload work records to Firebase. The choice is excluded from DashTrack manual/cloud backups and Android cloud backup/device transfer. When no choice is saved, the checkbox starts on, but RevenueCat is not initialized or called before your first explicit connection, restore or plan request. A successful explicit action with the choice on can save permission to reconnect and refresh at a later safe foreground startup. Manual actions leave a saved off choice off. Turning the choice on yourself also permits a connection now, when safely in the foreground.

Turn Remember billing connection off before requesting subscription-data deletion if you want to stop DashTrack's own automatic reconnection and status checks. This does not cancel a subscription, delete existing records or shut down an already configured SDK. RevenueCat may still make its own lifecycle requests while active; this control does not promise that all SDK network traffic stops. Explicit manual actions can still contact the service.

To request deletion of DashTrack's RevenueCat customer records, email dashtrackpro@gmail.com with the subject DashTrack Pro subscription data deletion. You do not need to reinstall the app or delete your Firebase account first. Explain that the request concerns subscription data. Support will help verify ownership and locate the correct anonymous subscriber record; a Google Play order reference may be needed. Do not send passwords, verification links, payment card details, private keys or work-record backups.

After ownership verification, support can request removal of the associated RevenueCat customer record. Necessary service/security/legal retention and Google's own purchase records may remain under the providers' policies. A later SDK connection, including an automatic connection if the remembered choice remains on, purchase or restore can create records again. This request does not remotely erase your device records or change the device's remembered connection choice.

Deletion does not cancel billing. Uninstalling, Firebase account deletion and RevenueCat customer deletion do not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop renewal, use Google Play subscription management. See the subscription terms and subscription privacy details.
