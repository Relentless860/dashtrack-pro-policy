Delete your DashTrack Pro account

Updated October 26, 2026

This page covers the optional email/password sign-in account in account-enabled Android 1.6.5 and later testing builds. It does not announce a public app release. It is not a request to delete your Google or delivery-platform account.

Request deletion without the app

Email dashtrackpro@gmail.com with the subject DashTrack Pro account deletion. Include the email address registered to your DashTrack account. Send from that address if possible.

Request account deletion

You do not need to reinstall the app or give a reason. If you cannot access the registered email, contact support and explain; additional ownership verification may be needed. Never send your password, verification code, recovery link or payment details.

How we handle the request

We review the request and verify control of the account before removing its Firebase sign-in record. Knowing an email address alone is not sufficient to request someone else's deletion. We will explain if further verification or a legitimate retention requirement affects the request. Support messages are handled by our email provider and retained only as reasonably needed for the request, recordkeeping or legal obligations.

What is deleted

Your DashTrack Firebase sign-in account, its account profile and ability to sign in with those credentials. Google processes removal from its live and backup systems under Firebase's documented retention and deletion practices; not all service logs or backups disappear immediately.

What stays on your device

This feature does not upload or attach shifts, mileage, receipts, earnings, settings or plans to a Firebase account. Those records are shared by the local app installation, not separated into private account profiles. Signing out or deleting a sign-in account does not hide or delete them. Support cannot remotely access or erase them.

Delete individual records in DashTrack, or clear app data in device settings to remove local installation data. Export anything you need first. Exports and backups you shared elsewhere are not remotely deleted by this request.

Delete from inside DashTrack

While safely parked with no active shift, open Driver settings → Account → Delete account. Enter your current password, type DELETE and confirm. Guest tracking remains available without creating another account.

Account creation and deletion do not start or cancel billing in this testing build; paid subscriptions are not enabled.
