DashTrack Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026
Last updated: October 12, 2026

DashTrack Pro is an offline shift, earnings, mileage, and tax-estimate tracker for independent delivery drivers. This policy describes how the current beta version of the app handles information.

Information you enter in the app

DashTrack Pro lets you choose to enter and store information about your work, including:

  • shift dates, start and end times, odometer readings, and business miles;
  • base pay, tips, bonuses, estimated vehicle cost per mile, and calculated earnings or mileage estimates;
  • delivery zones, weather conditions, and optional shift notes; and
  • app preferences, such as saved delivery zones and a default vehicle-cost estimate.

The app calculates summaries and tax-export information from the records you enter.

How your information is stored and used

In the current beta, your shift records and preferences are stored in a database on your device. The app uses them only to provide its tracking, calculation, insight, backup, and tax-export features.

The current app code does not send your shift records to DashTrack Pro servers, an advertising service, or an analytics service. DashTrack Pro does not offer user accounts, cloud synchronization, subscriptions, or in-app advertising in this beta.

Exports and sharing

You can choose to export a JSON backup or a CSV tax export. When you choose an export, DashTrack Pro creates the selected file temporarily in its app cache on your device and opens your device's system sharing interface. The temporary copy may remain until the app or operating system clears that cache. The app sends the file only to the app, person, service, or destination that you select.

Once you share an export, the recipient's privacy practices apply. Please review the contents before sharing because an export may include the shift and preference information described above.

Information we receive when you contact support

You do not need to provide your name, email address, or contact details to use the app. If you contact support at Dylanadamuska1995@gmail.com, we may receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address, name, device details, and description of the issue. We use that information only to respond to and help resolve your request. Messages are handled by our email provider and retained only as long as reasonably needed for support, recordkeeping, or legal obligations.

Information DashTrack Pro does not collect through the current app

The DashTrack Pro features in the current beta do not access location services, contacts, camera, microphone, health data, or advertising identifiers. The app code does not include behavioral tracking or third-party analytics. On some Android versions, the export and sharing feature may declare storage-related permissions; DashTrack Pro uses this capability to create the export you request and does not browse or collect your personal files. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

The app includes a link to the Internal Revenue Service mileage-rate webpage. If you open that link, the IRS website and your browser are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete individual shift records in the app. For all locally stored app data, you can delete the app from your device or clear its application data through your device settings. Export a backup first if you want to keep your records.

Because your records are stored locally in this beta, DashTrack Pro cannot retrieve, correct, or delete records from your device for you. Device backups, shared exports, and support emails may be subject to the settings and policies of your device, backup provider, email provider, or chosen sharing destination.

Security

We design DashTrack Pro so that the current beta keeps work records on your device rather than in a DashTrack Pro cloud account. You are responsible for using device protections, such as a screen lock and current operating-system updates, and for choosing where to share exported files. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

DashTrack Pro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can address the request.

Changes to this policy

If DashTrack Pro adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, new permissions, or other material data practices, we will update this policy before or when those practices take effect and revise the effective date above.
