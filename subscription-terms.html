DashTrack Pro subscriptions

Last updated November 3, 2026.

Paid subscriptions are not enabled in current free beta builds. This page describes the planned Google Play subscription flow; it does not create a payment, trial or subscription.

Free and premium access

Core shift tracking and access to your existing saved records remain available without a subscription. Premium adds delivery-planning and analysis tools, restaurant favorites, expense tools and ad-free access. The in-app offer identifies the included tools. Beta feature-preview access is separate from a paid subscription.

Prices and renewal

The Google Play offer and purchase sheet show the price in your store currency, billing period and any applicable tax or offer terms before purchase. Monthly and annual plans automatically renew at the price and period disclosed by Google Play unless cancelled. Annual plans charge the full annual amount, rather than a monthly installment. Prices and availability can differ by region.

Trials

A free trial is offered only when Google Play confirms eligibility and the app displays it. The displayed offer states the trial period and the recurring price that follows. If you accept a trial, it becomes a paid auto-renewing subscription unless cancelled before it ends. A free beta preview is not a subscription trial and does not automatically start a charge.

Restore, manage or cancel

Open Premium → Access & subscriptions to restore an existing purchase or open Google Play subscription management. Use the Google Play account that originally purchased the subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; access generally continues through the current paid period, subject to Google Play's status and any refund or revocation. For a trial, check the cancellation effect shown by Google Play.

Uninstalling DashTrack, signing out or deleting its optional Firebase account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel through Google Play before renewal to avoid the next charge. Firebase login does not itself transfer purchase ownership or back up local records.

Remembered billing connection

In a billing-enabled version, Remember billing connection starts on when no choice has been saved. Displaying this choice does not initialize or call RevenueCat before your first explicit Connect subscription status, Restore existing purchases or Load Google Play plans action. A successful action with the choice on saves permission to reconnect and refresh status after reopening the app in a safe foreground state. A saved off choice stays off during manual connection, restore and plan requests until you explicitly turn it on again. Turning it on yourself also permits a connection now, when the app is in a safe foreground state.

The remembered value is only an on/off choice in encrypted Android device storage, separate from verified premium access. It is not sent to Firebase or transferred in DashTrack manual/cloud backups or Android cloud backup/device transfer. Turning it off stops DashTrack's own automatic reconnection and checks; it does not cancel billing, delete subscription records or shut down an already configured RevenueCat SDK. The SDK may still perform its own lifecycle requests while active. You can still make explicit manual requests. Use Google Play to cancel renewal.

Payments, refunds and access

Google Play processes purchases and handles payment details. DashTrack does not receive your payment card number. For billing problems or refund eligibility, use Google Play refund support or contact DashTrack support. Applicable Google Play rules and consumer rights apply; this information does not limit rights provided by law.

Premium unlocks after verified purchase confirmation. Pending or declined payments do not grant new premium access. Online verification may be needed to restore or refresh access. Losing premium access does not delete saved work records; keep your manual backups, especially before switching devices or clearing app data.

Privacy and support

RevenueCat verifies Google Play subscription status using an anonymous app-user identifier, transaction and subscription information, and required device/app information. DashTrack does not attach shift earnings, GPS routes, receipts or email as RevenueCat subscriber attributes. See the privacy policy for processing and deletion details.

Contact dashtrackpro@gmail.com. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment card details or private service keys. Subscription access does not guarantee earnings, tax savings or a deduction.
